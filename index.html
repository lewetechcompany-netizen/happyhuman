FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I end strict mode early?

No – that's what makes it work. Strict mode ends only when its timer runs out. The only way around it is a full factory reset of the phone, which HappyHuman protects behind your PIN. Start it only when you're sure.

What happens when the 7-day trial ends?

You get a reminder the day before. After the trial, blocks pause until you choose Yearly or Lifetime. Reinstalling doesn't restart the trial.

Do you see what I browse?

No. All filtering runs on your device. We only store your account, your settings and your plan.

Why does the app need accessibility and VPN permissions?

The local VPN filters website lookups on the phone, and the accessibility service closes blocked apps, NSFW posts and attempts to switch protection off. Nothing leaves your phone.

Does one purchase cover my computer too?

Yes. Your plan is tied to your HappyHuman account, so the computer versions will use the same purchase when they launch.

Which languages are supported?

HappyHuman is in English by default, and you can switch language in settings.
