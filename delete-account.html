Privacy

Delete your HappyHuman account

In the app

  1. Open HappyHuman and enter your PIN.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account and confirm.

Your account, settings and plan information are deleted right away. (Not possible while strict mode is running.)

Without the app

E-mail lewetech.company@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

Sign-in account, block settings, strict/Zen times, block lists and plan records. Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google's policy.
