Refund policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Lewe Tech Oy, Finland

14-day money-back guarantee

If HappyHuman isn't right for you, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. This applies to both the Yearly plan and the Lifetime licence. No reason is needed.

How to request a refund

Email lewetech.company@gmail.com with the email address used for the purchase, or reply to your Paddle receipt. Purchases on the website and desktop apps are sold by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record, and refunds are paid by Paddle to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

Cancelling a yearly plan

You can cancel the Yearly plan at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and Premium stays active until the end of the paid year. Renewals are refundable within 14 days of the renewal date.

Google Play purchases

Purchases made in the Android app are handled by Google Play and follow Google Play's refund rules.

Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.
