Questions

Can I switch strict mode off early?

No – that's the point. Strict mode ends only when its time runs out. The only way around it is a full factory reset of the phone, which needs your HappyHuman PIN. Start it only when you're sure.

What happens when the 7-day trial ends?

You get a reminder the day before. After the trial, the blocks pause until you choose Yearly or Lifetime. Reinstalling the app doesn't restart the trial.

Do you see what I browse?

No. All filtering runs on your device. We only store your account, your settings and your plan. See the privacy policy.

Why does the app need accessibility and VPN permissions?

The local VPN filters website lookups on the phone, and the accessibility service closes blocked apps, NSFW posts and attempts to switch protection off. Nothing leaves your phone.

Does one purchase work on my computer too?

Yes. Your plan is tied to your HappyHuman account, so the computer versions use the same purchase when they launch.
