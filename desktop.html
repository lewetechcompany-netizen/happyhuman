Every browser, every app

A filter built into the system covers Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave and the apps on your computer – private windows too.

Safe mode forced

SafeSearch and YouTube Restricted Mode are switched on through the browsers' own settings, and secure-DNS tricks are blocked.

Apps closed

Casino, loan and social apps you've blocked – and VPN apps that try to get around the filter – are closed when they open.

Strict mode syncs

Start strict mode on your phone and your computer follows. It keeps running offline and doesn't care if the clock is changed.

Uninstall protection

Removing HappyHuman needs your PIN. During strict mode it can't be removed at all.

One purchase

Yearly (€9.99) or Lifetime (€24.99) covers your phone and your computers. Buy here on the website or in the app.

Per system

Windows 10 and 11: a background service that starts with the computer, filters website lookups for all network adapters and sets browser policies. Signed installer.

macOS 13 or newer: a signed and notarised app with Apple's network filter extension. You approve it once in System Settings.

Linux: a system service for Ubuntu, Fedora and other common distributions.

Admin rights are needed once during installation. Filtering stays on your computer – nothing about your browsing is sent to us.
