Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. Controller: Lewe Tech Oy, Tampere, Finland · lewetech.company@gmail.com

Summary

HappyHuman blocks content on your phone. All filtering happens on the device. We do not collect, store, sell or share your browsing history, the apps you use or what is on your screen.

Data we store

  • Account: your user ID and e-mail address from Google, Apple or e-mail sign-in (Firebase Authentication).
  • Settings: which blocks are on, strict mode and Zen mode end times, your own block list, app limits, Night Shield and payday settings (Cloud Firestore), so they come back after reinstalling.
  • Purchases: your plan and its end date. Payments are handled by Google Play (Android) and, for purchases on the website or computers, by Stripe; we never see card details.
  • On the phone only: your PIN as a salted hash and simple counters of blocked attempts. They never leave the phone.

VPN service

HappyHuman uses Android's VpnService only to create a local DNS filter on your phone. Only domain-name lookups pass through it; they are checked on the device and answered or forwarded to a family-safe public resolver (CleanBrowsing) or Cloudflare. We do not route, log or inspect your other traffic and we do not run any VPN servers.

Accessibility service

With your permission, the "HappyHuman Guard" accessibility service reads the browser address bar and the text on screen of the apps you chose to block, to close blocked apps, blocked sites and adult posts, and to stop the app from being removed or reset while your blocks are on. This happens only on your phone; nothing on your screen is recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Device administrator

Used only to prevent uninstalling HappyHuman while your blocks are on and to lock the screen if someone tries to remove that protection.

Legal basis and retention

We process account and settings data to provide the service you asked for (contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). Data is kept while you have an account and deleted within 30 days after you delete it.

Processors

Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore; EU/US with standard contractual clauses) and Google Play Billing.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data and object to processing. Delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or see how to delete your account. You may complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Children

HappyHuman is meant for adults (18+).

Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
